Zahlungsauftrag

Reimbursement form

Fill in your expenses below. You'll get the official ZHdK payment order, ready for the finance staff, and a preview to check your entries. You don't need Adobe Acrobat.

Your data stays in your browser: nothing is uploaded or stored. Only when you convert a currency, the purchase date is sent to the Swiss customs office (BAZG) to look up the official rate.

    Payout currency Auszahlungs-Währung

    All amounts must be in the payout currency. If you paid in another currency and your bank did not officially convert the amount into CHF or EUR, it must be converted at the official exchange rate of the date of purchase.

    For such an expense, tick "Paid in another currency" on its line and enter what you paid: the amount is converted automatically at the official BAZG daily rate ↗, and the calculation is printed on the receipt page.

    One line per receipt. For each line, upload the receipt (Beleg) and the matching payment from your bank account (Kontobewegung). Photos and PDFs both work, up to 10 lines.

    Your documents are numbered automatically: line 1 becomes Beleg 1, and each receipt is placed on one page together with its proof of payment. This lets the finance team refer to a specific document if something needs correcting.
